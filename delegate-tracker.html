Who's winning Maine's 500 delegate seats

Updated July 18, 2026, 6:00 p.m.
We're tracking delegates actually elected at county caucuses, as results are certified by the Maine Democratic Party.

A delegate is counted here only once their county's official results list them as an elected regular delegate -- not an alternate. Alternates are tracked separately below, since they only get a convention vote if a regular delegate can't attend.

A delegate's candidate is whichever Senate hopeful they endorsed in their filed candidacy statement, or the campaign whose official slate they ran on. Results come from each county's certified vote count (ElectionBuddy reports or the state party's posted results).

This is a rolling count -- early counties to report are not necessarily representative of the full 500-seat outcome.
